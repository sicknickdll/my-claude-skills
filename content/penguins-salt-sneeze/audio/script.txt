Did you know?
Penguins can drink *seawater.*
For us, salt water means *thirst.*
Their secret? A *salt gland*
right above each eye.
It pulls *salt* out of the blood,
like a tiny built-in *filter.*
The salty drops drip out the *nostrils,*
and they *sneeze* them away!
Achoo! Salt *gone.*
Follow for more *weird animals!*
