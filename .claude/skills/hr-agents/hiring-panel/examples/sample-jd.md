# Medior Frontend Developer (React), Ghent

Northlight Labs (fictional) builds booking software for 400 sports clubs in Belgium and the Netherlands. Our product team of 25 ships weekly, and we're looking for a medior frontend developer to own parts of our customer-facing web app.

## What you'll do

- Build and ship features in React and TypeScript, from design hand-off to production
- Improve performance and accessibility of a large single-page app (Core Web Vitals, WCAG 2.1 AA)
- Contribute to our component library and design system together with our designer
- Review code, write tests (Jest, Playwright) and help junior developers grow

## What you bring

- 3+ years of professional experience with React and TypeScript
- A solid grasp of modern CSS, responsive design and accessibility
- Experience with automated testing and CI
- Dutch at C1 level or higher, and good English
- Based in Belgium, able to work from our Ghent office two days a week

## Nice to have

- Next.js, GraphQL, Storybook
- Experience with performance monitoring
