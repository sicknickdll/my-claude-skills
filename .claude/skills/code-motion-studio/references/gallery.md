# Gallery and prompt libraries

Examples from the source thread (all posted Sep 2026, all unverified here). Use them to pick a reference medium or constraint for a new brief.

**Hand-drawn and painted**
- Watercolour music video "Claude-Pop - I'm Upping My P(Doom)": open source at `github.com/JohnHeibel/PDoomVideo`.
- 250 years of U.S. history as a sand-painting animation, with voiceover and sound effects, from one prompt (Michael Guo).

**UI and launch**
- A slow, continuous-camera product teaser in HyperFrames, from a dictated "rambled" prompt (Jake Moran).

**Explainers**
- "How browsers work" in 40 s, every frame in JavaScript (Addy Osmani).
- Cocktail recipe motion graphic from a single photo, rendered in HTML (Rory Flynn); prompt lets Claude choose "javascript or html (w/e you think will produce the best)".
- Black-Scholes explained with voiceover beside a generic Subway Surfers-style game (Riley Goodside).

**Generative**
- Gold-leaf mosaic whose tiles flock into figures: WebGL2, no libraries (Chris Riley).
- Pixel-art wizard at 128×96 px with a fixed 24-colour palette (Majid Manzarpour).

**Other**
- Short film "Everyone sends Claude their requests, but one girl sends a question instead: 'What do you love?'" drawn in JavaScript (Kevin Ngo).
- Playable riverboat through a Japanese valley with weather and day/night, Three.js (Meng To).

**Prompt libraries (per thread)**
- `X-RayLuan/awesome-opus-5-5-video-prompts` (48 examples, verbatim prompts)
- `jacobbubu/claude-opus-5-5-js-animation-research` (30 cases ranked by views, with methods)
- `anthropics/skills` (official skills, including `algorithmic-art` for p5.js)

Guidance echoed in the thread from Anthropic's frontend-design skill: "Critique your own work as you build, taking screenshots if your environment supports it", and "sometimes less is more; extra animation contributes to the feeling that the design is AI-generated."
